Recipe #1: French Fries

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: with ketchup
Ingredients Quantity
Potato 5 pieces
Salt 2 spoons
Pepper 1 spoon
Oil 2 liters

Step 1: Cut potatos in rectangle slices. Boil oil to 200 degrees celcius.

Step 2: Add salt & pepper, then mix it all.

Step 3: Put potato slices in boiling oil.

Step 4: Wait for 5 minutes and get ready French Fries. Serve with ketchup for better taste!

Recipe #2: Hot cocoa drink

  • Time: 7 minutes
  • Serves: with marshmello
Ingredients Quantity
Cocoa powder 20 gramm
Water 300 ml
Sugar 1 spoon

Step 1: Boil water to 90-95 degrees celcius.

Step 2: Add cocoa powder and sugar in cup.

Step 3: Add hot water.

Step 4: Mix well everything with the spoon and it's ready to drink. Add marshmello optional.

Recipe #3: Simple sandwich

  • Time: 5 minutes
  • Serves: with tea
Ingredients Quantity
Bread 2 slices
Ham 3 slices
Tomato 4 slices
Sause mayonnaise and ketchup
Lettuce 2 leafs

Step 1: Cut ingredients in slices with size that you prefer.

Step 2: Mix couple of spoons of mayonnaise and ketchup to make a sause.

Step 3: Add sause on both bread slices.

Step 4: Add ingredientstogether in following order: bread, ham, tomato, bread. Your sandwich is done! Get hot tea for better experience!
